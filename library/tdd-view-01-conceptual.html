Draft status

Written 2026-11-09 from the source ledger. Only S2, S4, S8, S9 and the OWASP list in S7 were read. Capabilities C1 to C4 come from IBM's account of Gartner guidance (S8). The structure used here follows the four-layer view and keeps the four pillars as a crosswalk. That choice is the architect's convenience, not a finding. See Debate 1 in CLAIMS-AND-DEBATES.

Problem and purpose

  • AI systems fail in ways conventional controls do not cover: they behave non-deterministically, depend on data that drifts, and accept natural-language input that can carry attacks [S7]. AI TRiSM exists to give an organization one set of controls for the trust, risk and security of those systems across their lifecycle [S8] [S9].
  • Without it, nobody can say which AI systems exist, what data they touch, whether they still behave as approved, or what stops them acting outside their remit. The loser is whoever is accountable when an AI system leaks data, causes harm, or fails an audit: the executive owner, the security team, and the people affected.

Actors

Actor Role Goal Trust level
AI risk owner Accountable executive or governance board Set risk tolerance, approve AI use cases Trusted, policy-setting
Model builder ML engineer or data scientist Train, evaluate and release models Trusted, constrained by release gates
Application owner Team shipping an AI-enabled application Deliver a feature without breaching policy Trusted, monitored
Security operations SOC and incident response Detect and contain AI-specific attacks Trusted, privileged
Privacy and compliance officer Maps obligations to controls Show conformance to NIST, ISO/IEC 42001 and law Trusted, read-heavy
End user Person prompting an AI system Get a useful answer Untrusted input source
AI agent Software acting for a user or process Complete tasks using tools and data Untrusted by default, least privilege
Third-party model or tool provider External model, API or plugin vendor Sell capability Untrusted, supply-chain risk [S7]
Auditor or regulator External reviewer Verify controls and evidence External, demands evidence

Capabilities

C1 to C4 are the four features IBM lists from Gartner guidance released in February 2025 [S8]. C5 to C8 are derived from the four pillars [S9] and the NIST trustworthiness characteristics [S2].

# Capability Outcome it delivers Actors Source
C1 AI catalog One inventory of every model, application and agent in use, including shadow AI AI risk owner, application owner [S8]
C2 AI data mapping Knowledge of which data each AI system reads, trains on and emits Privacy officer, model builder [S8]
C3 Continuous assurance and evaluation Repeated testing that systems still meet their approved behavior Model builder, security operations [S8]
C4 Runtime inspection and enforcement Policy applied to inputs, outputs and actions while the system runs Security operations, application owner [S8] [S9]
C5 Explainability and monitoring Ability to interpret decisions and detect drift, bias and anomalous behavior Model builder, auditor [S9] [S2]
C6 Model lifecycle governance (ModelOps) Controlled deployment, versioning, retraining and retirement Model builder, AI risk owner [S9]
C7 Information and privacy governance Sensitive data protected in training, prompts, retrieval and outputs Privacy officer [S9]
C8 Compliance mapping and evidence Controls tied to NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001 and applicable law, with evidence kept Compliance officer, auditor [S2] [S4]

Key concepts (conceptual data)

Concept Meaning Related to
AI asset Any model, application, agent or dataset under governance C1, C2
Risk Probability of an event and the size of its consequence, as NIST defines it [S2] C3, C8
Trustworthiness characteristic One of seven NIST properties: valid and reliable, safe, secure and resilient, accountable and transparent, explainable and interpretable, privacy-enhanced, fair with harmful bias managed [S2] C5, C7, C8
Policy A rule the organization wants enforced on AI use C4, C8
Control A mechanism that implements a policy C3 to C7
Evidence A record showing a control ran and what it found C8
Agency The degree to which an AI system can act on its own through tools [S7] C4
