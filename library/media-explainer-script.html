AI-TRISM explainer, architect edition

Pack: 00 - AI-TRISM Hub · Argument: CLAIMS-AND-DEBATES · Ledger: source-ledger

Business Explanation

A 75 second narrated film for an architect about to walk into a design review. It says what AI TRiSM is, why the sources disagree on its structure, what to anchor a design to, and where the claims run out. Every line is tied to a ledger source or marked unverified. Nothing here comes from the debates the pack has not yet tested.
