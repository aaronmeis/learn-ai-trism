Business Explanation

A narrated film for an architect walking into a design review. It says what AI TRiSM is and what Gartner actually publishes, walks the conceptual, logical and physical views and the AI data path as animated diagrams, maps the views into TOGAF, DoDAF and UAF, and ends on the decision the BLUF asks for. Every line is tied to a ledger source or marked unverified.

Scenes

# Scene Visual Narration (voiced phrases)
1 The problem NotebookLM slide 1 Unmanaged AI on one side. A controlled path on the other. AI TRiSM is Gartner's name for the controls that build that path.
2 Why old controls miss slide 2 Conventional controls assume fixed code and predictable output. AI answers probabilistically, and takes plain language that can carry an attack. The deck's answer: treat the model as an untrusted user.
3 What Gartner publishes cards Four layers of technical capabilities, supporting governance, trustworthiness, fairness, safety, reliability, security, privacy and data protection. It does not name the layers.
4 Three shapes in the wild cards, 3 columns Vendors: four pillars, or four layers, both citing a paywalled Market Guide. A scholarly review adds a fifth pillar, adversarial resilience.
5 Conceptual view animated Mermaid Seven actors act on one system with eight capabilities, from AI catalog to compliance evidence, out to the auditor.
6 Eight capabilities slide 4 First four from IBM's account of Gartner's guidance. The deck stacks all eight against NIST's four functions and treats external models and agents with zero trust.
7 Logical view animated Mermaid Our proposal. Prompt, runtime gateway, policy engine, model, identity broker, evidence.
8 Policy outside the model slide 5 Policy enforcement sits outside the model. The model never enforces its own rules.
9 Physical view animated Mermaid Five trust zones. Red links carry untrusted content. Vendors, SKUs and regions unverified.
10 The outside world animated Mermaid Outside systems around the platform. Gov-cloud unverified on every row.
11 The AI data path animated Mermaid Eight control points, CP1 to CP8.
12 Three architecture languages cards, 3 columns The same views map to TOGAF phases, DoDAF viewpoints and UAF cells. The crosswalk keeps all three.
13 What to anchor to slide 3 NIST voluntary. ISO 42001 certifiable via independent bodies. NIST crosswalk between them. EU AI Act binding. No source supports certifying against AI TRiSM.
14 Are guardrails enough slide 9 OWASP: seven mitigations, about two filter, about five limit privilege or add process. Academic talk: outer layer, red-team first. Vendor keynote: filters can't cover infinite inputs, agent identity, speaker sells detection. Nobody measured detection rates.
15 Risks the pillar names miss cards AI 600-1 names twelve risks. Several fit awkwardly under pillar names alone. The mapping is our draft.
16 What we don't know slide 12 The deck's register of unknowns, plus ours: paywalled Market Guide, unrun guardrail lab test, gov-cloud unverified.
17 Order of work slide 13 Visibility first, then control, then enforcement.
18 The decision asked cards Approve the vendor-neutral architecture as the baseline. Capability map plus ISO 42001 and NIST anchors. Defer product bake-offs.
