Block 6: Spaced review

Block 6: closed-book retrieval and the multi-pass log for AI TRiSM.

Time: 20 minutes Goal: Retrieve without rereading, score the attempt, and schedule the next pass. Pack: AI-TRISM hub

Business Explanation

Business Explanation

Twenty minutes of closed-book recall is the quality check on the day. The score goes into the progress log with the specific misses, so the next study pass repairs the defense instead of replaying the whole pack.

Sources

  • Quiz items: quiz-bank. Do not open the folded answers until the sheet is finished.
  • Log: Progress Log#Multi-pass tracker.
  • Method: 03 - Multi-Pass Progress Tracker.
  • Misses already starred in blocks 3, 4, and 5.

Actions

  1. 2 minutes. Close every block note. Keep only a blank page.
  2. 12 minutes. Answer the pull list below. One to three sentences each. No ledger.
  3. 4 minutes. Open the quiz bank folds for those item numbers only. Mark right or wrong.
  4. 2 minutes. Write the score and the misses into the Progress Log. Set a Study 2 date.

How to log

On the multi-pass table in Progress Log:

Column What to write today
Notes Date, if the block note was actually studied. Leave blank if you only sat the quiz.
Study Date for any block you practiced before this quiz.
Quiz The X/8 on each block you pulled, or one score in block 6's Quiz cell plus the per-block misses.
Study 2 A date. Weak score, under 6/8, about two days out. Strong score, 7/8 or 8/8, about a week out.
Study 3 Leave blank until Study 2 happens. Typical gap after that is two to three weeks.
Missed-it Terms and traps only. Example: "layer names tagged primary", "called NIST a certificate", "quoted Ray 2026".

Do not tick the pedagogy-day checkboxes in the Progress Log unless you finished that block today. This pack's build checkboxes are separate. They are not your study score.

Retrieval check

The pull list is the retrieval check. Folded answers live in the quiz bank so this page cannot spoil the attempt.

When do you reopen a block note today?
Answer

After the eight answers are written. Use the note only to understand a miss, then write the miss in Missed-it. Do not reread a block you got right.

What date rule does the progress log use?
Answer

A weak score comes back within a couple of days. A high score can wait longer. The method note says typical gaps of about two days, then about one week, then about two to three weeks 03 - Multi-Pass Progress Tracker.

Exit criteria

  • Eight answers exist on paper, written closed-book.
  • Score is in Progress Log as a fraction.
  • Missed-it cells contain words, or the word "none" if the item was clean.
  • Study 2 date is a calendar day, not "later".
