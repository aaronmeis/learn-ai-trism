Business Explanation

The bank is the closed-book test for the one-day pack. Items check the market term, the standards anchor, the control placement, and the claims the pack still refuses to make. Scores go in the Progress Log, and each answer says why a miss would hurt in a design review.

Answer closed-book. Grade after you expand the folds. Log the score in Progress Log#Multi-pass tracker. Each item has a stable id in a comment before it. The study console at learn-ai-trism uses the same ids.

Block 6 pulls items from blocks 1 to 5 (the P1 to P8 list in 06-spaced-review). Do not preview those folds on the study morning.

Block 1: Core concepts

  1. What does the August 2025 Gartner Hype Cycle press release actually say about AI TRiSM's structure?
Answer

Four layers of technical capabilities; outcomes include governance, trustworthiness, fairness, safety, reliability, security, privacy, and data protection. The PR does not name the four layers [S1].

Why it matters. This is the sentence a reviewer with the press release open will test. Four layers is confirmed. Names are not [S1].

Trap. Reading "four layers" as "the four layers we use are Gartner's." The release lists outcomes (governance, trustworthiness, fairness, safety, reliability, security, privacy, data protection) and names no layer [S1].

  1. List NIST AI RMF's four functions and seven trustworthiness characteristics.
Answer

Functions: Govern, Map, Measure, Manage. Characteristics: valid and reliable, safe, secure and resilient, accountable and transparent, explainable and interpretable, privacy-enhanced, fair with harmful bias managed. The framework is voluntary [S2].

Why it matters. The functions are the vocabulary for who does what. The seven characteristics are the list the pillars get tested against in Debate 4 [S2].

Trap. Calling AI RMF mandatory or certifiable. It is voluntary [S2].

  1. Who certifies an organization to ISO/IEC 42001, and what certification route did the desk pass find for NIST?
Answer

Independent certification bodies certify the AIMS; ISO does not. ANAB accredits the bodies [S14] [S15]. No NIST certification scheme was found for AI RMF 1.0 or AI 600-1; both are voluntary [S2] [S3].

Why it matters. A sponsor who says "ISO certified us" will repeat it to an auditor, and the first follow-up is which accredited certification body issued the certificate [S14] [S15].

Trap. Saying ISO or NIST certifies. ISO does not certify organizations, and no NIST certification scheme was found for AI RMF 1.0 or AI 600-1 [S2] [S3].

Block 2: Mental models

  1. In this pack, what job do pillars do, and what job do layers do?
Answer

Pillars are the secondary application crosswalk: explainability, ModelOps, application security, privacy [S9] [S10]. Layers are the placement map. Their names are also secondary. The Gartner press release confirms only the count of four layers [S1]. The preference for layers is Debate 1's convenience, not a finding.

Why it matters. Giving each word one job lets a single diagram carry both: layers say where a control sits, pillars say which application concern it serves [S9] [S10].

Trap. Calling the preference for layers a finding. It is the architect's convenience in Debate 1, not evidence.

  1. After Debate 2, what is the design anchored to, and what role does TRiSM keep?
Answer

Anchor requirements to ISO/IEC 42001 and risk language to NIST AI RMF. TRiSM remains the capability and tool-placement map. E2A found no design that uses TRiSM as the sole auditable anchor. "Auditable" fits the ISO certification-body route better than NIST, which is voluntary [S2] [S14].

Why it matters. "Auditable" fits the ISO certification-body route better than NIST, which is voluntary. ISO/IEC 42001 gives the requirements, NIST gives the risk language, and TRiSM keeps the placement-map job [S2] [S14].

Trap. Anchoring the design to TRiSM alone. E2A found no design that uses TRiSM as the sole auditable anchor.

  1. Of the seven OWASP LLM01:2025 mitigations, about how many are filtering, and what does that do to a gateway-only design?
Answer

About two are filtering or output validation. About five are privilege, architecture, or process [S7b]. A gateway stays on the design; it does not replace least privilege, human approval, segregation of external content, or adversarial testing.

Why it matters. A design that leans on the gateway alone overlooks that about five of the seven LLM01 mitigations are privilege, architecture, or process [S7b]. That is the case for keeping the identity broker.

Trap. Quoting a detection rate for the gateway. E3W has not been run and no ledger source measures one.

Block 3: Active practice

  1. On Handbook Assistant, which secondary layer owns the ticket tool's privilege, and which primary control text supports that choice?
Answer

Infrastructure and stack, name from [S9], secondary. The control text is OWASP LLM01 privilege control and least privilege [S7b], with agent identity from the CSA talk [S12].

Why it matters. Privilege is a stack decision. The ticket tool gets its own identity that can draft a ticket and cannot touch payroll, which is least privilege made concrete [S7b] [S12].

Trap. Putting tool privilege under runtime inspection. The gateway can inspect the call, but who may make it is the identity's job.

  1. Which OWASP LLM 2025 entry's mitigation list does this pack quote in full, and what does block 3 still say that the ledger has moved past?
Answer

LLM01 Prompt Injection, seven mitigations [S7b]. The ledger now marks the other entries as read too ([S7c], [S7d]), but the desk passes record only one-line control themes for them, not full mitigation lists. Block 3 still says only LLM01 was read in full, which is older than those ledger rows.

Why it matters. Know exactly what you read before you say "OWASP says". LLM01 is the entry the pack can walk mitigation by mitigation [S7b].

Trap. Inventing mitigations for entries you only know by name, or repeating block 3's older count without checking the ledger.

  1. Which NIST function owns a pre-release red team, and is the evaluation harness a NIST diagram?
Answer

Measure owns the test. Manage owns the release decision that uses the result [S2]. The harness is a proposal on the TDD logical view. Bo Li's talk supports red teaming first [S11]. It is not a box in the AI RMF figure.

Why it matters. Measure runs the test and Manage decides on the release, so a red-team result with no release owner is a number nobody acts on [S2].

Trap. Treating the evaluation harness as a box in the AI RMF figure. It is a proposal on the TDD logical view.

  1. Which NIST AI RMF function owns the pre-release red-team test, and which owns the release decision?
  • A. Govern owns the test, and Map owns the release decision.
  • B. Measure owns the test, and Manage owns the release decision.
  • C. Map owns the test, and Govern owns the release decision.
  • D. Manage owns the test, and Measure owns the release decision.
Answer

B. Measure owns the test, and Manage owns the release decision.

  • A. Govern is the cross-cutting culture and policy function, not the test owner.
  • B. Right. Measure owns the test and Manage owns the decision that uses the result [S2].
  • C. Map frames context and risks before testing starts.
  • D. The roles are reversed. Manage acts on results that Measure produces.

Why it matters. A red-team result with no release owner is a number nobody acts on [S2].

Trap. Swapping Measure and Manage because both sound like testing.

Block 4: Teach it

  1. What four authorities does the five-minute talk separate, in order?
Answer

Market: AI TRiSM [S1]. Voluntary: NIST AI RMF and AI 600-1 [S2] [S3]. Certifiable: ISO/IEC 42001 [S14]. Statute: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 [S6].

Why it matters. These four headings are the only sheet you may hold in the five-minute talk. Each means a different consequence for the design: market term, voluntary, certifiable, law.

Trap. Blurring two of them. TRiSM briefed as a standard, or NIST briefed as a certificate, is the over-claim reviewers jump on.

  1. A reviewer says "Gartner named the layers governance, runtime, information, and infrastructure." What do you answer?
Answer

The free press release says four layers and does not name them [S1]. Those names come from secondary pages that cite the Market Guide [S9]. The Market Guide PDF is paywalled. E1B is partial.

Why it matters. This is the most likely correction in the room. Agreeing out of politeness hands the reviewer a claim they can falsify [S1] [S9].

Trap. Agreeing to keep the meeting moving. The names come from secondary pages that cite a paywalled Market Guide.

  1. What is the 45-second answer to "certify the program to TRiSM"?
Answer

The ledger shows no TRiSM certification path. Point the certificate at ISO/IEC 42001 through an independent certification body [S14] [S15]. Use TRiSM to place tools. NIST remains the voluntary risk vocabulary [S2].

Why it matters. A short, sourced refusal moves the conversation to what can be certified: ISO/IEC 42001 [S14] [S15].

Trap. Saying "we are TRiSM certified." The ledger shows no TRiSM certification path.

Block 5: Edge cases

  1. What order does Bo Li give for an outer AI firewall?
Answer

Risk assessment and red teaming first. If the model cannot be fundamentally fixed, add an outer AI firewall or guard rail [S11]. The talk does not supply a detection rate.

Why it matters. The order is the argument: test before you add a wrapper. A firewall is the fallback when the model cannot be fixed [S11].

Trap. Starting with the firewall. The talk gives no detection rate either, so none can be quoted.

  1. What limit does the CSA keynote state, and what interest must you label?
Answer

Filters and rules cannot cover an infinite number of inputs and outputs. The talk pushes agent identity and over-permissioning [S12]. The speaker's employer sells behavioral detection. That interest stays attached to the quote.

Why it matters. The stated limit supports privilege and identity controls, and the speaker's employer sells behavioral detection. Quote the first with the second attached [S12].

Trap. Quoting the talk without the vendor interest.

  1. Name three claims this pack still cannot make.
Answer

Any three from the claims note: Gartner-owned names for the four layers, Gartner-owned names for the four pillars, a Gartner-owned pillar definition set (E4A), a measured guardrail detection rate (E3W is unrun), or a certification path for TRiSM itself CLAIMS-AND-DEBATES.

Why it matters. Knowing what the pack cannot claim is the other half of defending it. Each refusal pairs with an over-claim a reviewer would otherwise let through.

Trap. Using an older list. The claims note was rewritten after the desk passes, so check its current "still cannot claim" section.

  1. What order does Bo Li give for adding an outer AI firewall?
  • A. Add the firewall first, then run a red team to tune it.
  • B. Risk assessment and red teaming first. If the model cannot be fundamentally fixed, add an outer firewall or guard rail.
  • C. Skip the red team when a firewall is in place.
  • D. Add the firewall only after a regulator asks for one.
Answer

B. Risk assessment and red teaming first. If the model cannot be fundamentally fixed, add an outer firewall or guard rail.

  • A. The talk puts testing first. The firewall is the fallback.
  • B. Right. Test first, wrap when the model cannot be fixed [S11].
  • C. The talk does not say a firewall replaces the red team.
  • D. Nothing in the talk ties the firewall to a regulator's request.

Why it matters. The order is the argument. It also means the talk supplies no detection rate [S11].

Trap. Starting with the firewall because it is the visible control.

Block 6: Spaced review

  1. State the four secondary layer names and the source grade of each name.
Answer

AI governance, AI runtime inspection and enforcement, information governance, infrastructure and stack. All four names are secondary, attributed to the Market Guide by pages such as Zscaler [S9]. The primary Gartner press release confirms the number four and not the names [S1].

Why it matters. Naming the layers is fine. Presenting the names as primary is not [S1] [S9].

Trap. Giving the names without their grade.

  1. Give the EU AI Act general application date and the Art. 6(1) date recorded in the desk pass.
Answer

General application 2 Aug 2026. Art. 6(1) high-risk path 2 Aug 2027. Earlier phases are recorded as 2 Feb 2025 and 2 Aug 2025 [S6].

Why it matters. Dates are what a reviewer checks against EUR-Lex. Phased application means "in force" needs a date and an article [S6].

Trap. Saying the Act is fully in force for every high-risk system. Art. 6(1) is a later date, and this use case is not classified under an annex.

  1. Which debate does the pack leave open on pillar coverage, and what evidence is still missing?
Answer

Debate 4, whether the pillars cover NIST's seven characteristics. E4A is partial: there are no Gartner-owned pillar definitions. The AI 600-1 suggested-action tables are only partly used. E4W has run: NIST does not require one separate control per characteristic [S2] [S3].

Why it matters. Naming the open debate shows you know where the evidence ends [S2] [S3].

Trap. Saying E4W is still unrun. It ran on 2026-11-09, though older block notes still say otherwise. Also avoid saying the pillars cover fairness and safety: the press-release outcomes name them, but the map from outcomes to pillars is unfinished.

  • B. 2 August 2026

Block 7: Application

  1. Name five tool categories on the one-pager and the layer or zone for each.
Answer

Asset registry in governance. Runtime gateway in runtime inspection. Agent identity broker in infrastructure, runtime zone. Evaluation harness in the build zone, serving Measure. Evidence store in governance, serving the AIMS. Data map and classifier also counts, under information governance. Component boundaries are a TDD proposal.

Why it matters. Categories are what a vendor-neutral one-pager can promise. The layer or zone is where a reviewer can challenge you.

Trap. Writing a product where the category belongs. Component boundaries are a TDD proposal, not a product list.

  1. What do you hand an auditor as the conformance anchor, and what do you hand them as the threat list?
Answer

Conformance anchor: ISO/IEC 42001 evidence, with the NIST crosswalk for control ids [S4] [S14]. Threat list: OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications 2025, with mitigations quoted in full only for LLM01 [S7] [S7b].

Why it matters. An auditor wants one anchor for conformance and one list for threats, kept apart [S4] [S7] [S7b] [S14].

Trap. Handing over TRiSM as the conformance anchor, or presenting OWASP as a certifiable standard.

  1. What sentence keeps the one-pager vendor-neutral when someone asks which product to buy?
Answer

Name the category and the zone, and leave the SKU to a later selection. Example: a runtime gateway in the runtime zone and an identity broker beside it. Gov-cloud availability is unverified in the TDD physical view. The design has to make sense before a brand is chosen.

Why it matters. "Which product?" arrives in every review. Category plus zone is an answer that stays true when the product changes.

Trap. Filling a SKU or gov-cloud cell from memory. The cell says UNVERIFIED.
