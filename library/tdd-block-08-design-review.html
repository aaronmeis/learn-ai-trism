Block 8: Design review and teach-back

Block 8 study guide: design-review teach-back across the five views.

Time: 30 minutes

Business Explanation

Business Explanation

The value of the day is a person who can brief the design without notes and without over-claiming. This block is that briefing. It asks for three decisions you will defend with a source, one decision you will not defend, and a closed-book sample from the quiz bank. Passing it means the views changed what you would say in a review, not that you finished reading.

Goal

Deliver an eight-minute teach-back that moves from capabilities to zones to failure, and score at least three quiz items with the notes closed.

Required reading

  • Altitude callouts only, one each, in Conceptual view, Logical view, Physical view, Integration and AI, and Detailed design.
  • The "can claim" and "cannot claim" lists in CLAIMS-AND-DEBATES.
  • Three items you choose from quiz-bank, after you have drafted the talk. Grade from the folded answers only when you are done.

What to learn

The teach-back has a fixed spine so you do not wander into a product pitch.

  1. Term (about one minute). AI TRiSM is a market name for technical capabilities around trust, risk, and security [S1] [S8]. It is not a management-system standard.
  2. Structure (about one minute). You place controls on four layers whose names you attribute to secondary sources [S9]. You keep four pillars as a crosswalk [S9] [S10]. You do not say Gartner's primary text listed those names. [S1] confirms "four layers" unnamed.
  3. Anchor (about one minute). Conformance conversations go to ISO/IEC 42001, which independent bodies can certify [S14] [S15]. Risk language goes to NIST AI RMF, which is voluntary [S2] [S3]. The crosswalk connects them [S4]. TRiSM tells you where tools sit. It does not certify you.
  4. One request (about three minutes). Untrusted prompt, policy outside the model, untrusted retrieval, scoped tool call through a broker that survives the gateway, output check, async evidence. Point at C4, C7, and C8 as you go. This is Logical view plus CP3 to CP7.
  5. What you will not defend (about two minutes). Primary Gartner layer names. Government-cloud fitness. A detection rate. That the four pillars cover safety and fairness [S2]. That enforce mode is latency-free. That E3W was run.

Altitude in one sentence each, which is the other half of the oral:

View One sentence
Conceptual Outcomes and actors only.
Logical Proposed parts, records, and flows. No hosts.
Physical Five zones and a standards profile. SKUs left unknown.
Integration Outside systems by category, and eight control points on the data path.
Detailed design A dependency order for standing the reference up. No packages.

TOGAF and DoDAF labels live in each view's framework lens. You need one example, not the whole matrix. A clean one: conceptual work is TOGAF Phase A and B and a DoDAF OV-1. Physical zones are Phase D and an SV-1 style resource picture. If you cannot remember the viewpoint number, say the altitude instead of guessing a code.

Defend these three. They are sourced.

  • Catalog before runtime blocking, because you cannot enforce on assets you have not listed [S8], and the build order says so.
  • Policy is not inside the model [S7].
  • ISO/IEC 42001 is a real certification route and TRiSM is not [S14] [S15].

Do not defend these, even if the room wants a yes.

  • "Gartner's four layers are named X, Y, Z, and W" as a primary quote.
  • Any gov-cloud row.
  • "The firewall stops prompt injection" without the privilege mitigations [S7b] and without E3W.

Score rule for the quiz sample: closed book, then open quiz-bank answers. Log the fraction in Progress Log on the view rows you actually quizzed. A weak score comes back in a later study pass. Do not quiz all eighteen items in this half hour.

Actions

  1. Speak the five-part spine once, timed. Cut anything that names a SKU.
  2. Write the three defensible claims and the three refusals on one card, each refusal paired with the over-claim it blocks.
  3. Answer three quiz items closed-book, at least one from view 2 or view 4 and one cross-cutting. Grade and log.
  4. If a view line still says a page was unread and the ledger says it was read, mention the tension. Do not stop to edit the view unless you meant to. This block is the talk.

Retrieval check

Closed book

Give the one-sentence altitude change from conceptual to logical to physical. Name one claim you will defend with a source, and one you will not.

Answer

Conceptual names outcomes and actors. Logical names proposed parts and flows without hosts. Physical names zones and standards, and leaves SKUs unknown. Defend: ISO/IEC 42001 is certifiable through independent bodies and is the conformance anchor [S14] [S15]. Do not defend a primary Gartner list of layer names, or a government-cloud claim, or a measured guardrail catch rate.

Exit criteria

  • Teach-back delivered against Conceptual view through Detailed design.
  • Three sourced claims and three explicit non-claims written.
  • At least three quiz-bank items scored closed-book and logged in Progress Log.
  • No W8, NotebookLM, publish, or install started from this block.
