Identity and access

  • Every AI agent gets its own identity, not a shared service account. A talk in the ledger asks "who is my agent, what can it access" as the starting question [S12].
  • Least privilege and just-in-time access apply to agents [S12]. OWASP recommends keeping the model's privileges to the minimum its task needs and handling extended functions in code, not in the model [S7].
  • Human approval for high-risk actions [S7].
  • Admin roles for the policy engine and evidence store should be separated from model builders. Proposal.
  • UNVERIFIED: how the identity provider federates with each third-party model provider.
