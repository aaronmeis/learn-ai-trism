Block 1: Core concepts

Block 1 study note: what AI TRiSM is, and how it sits beside NIST, ISO/IEC 42001, and the EU AI Act.

Time: 60 minutes Goal: State what AI TRiSM is, which lists are secondary, and which documents carry voluntary, certifiable, or legal weight. Pack: AI-TRISM hub

What the term is

AI TRiSM means AI trust, risk, and security management. Gartner owns the term. The August 2025 Hype Cycle press release says AI TRiSM comprises four layers of technical capabilities. The outcomes it lists are governance, trustworthiness, fairness, safety, reliability, security, privacy, and data protection [S1].

A later Gartner article, partly gated, treats TRiSM as technical controls that sit beyond policy [S16]. That is the strongest Gartner-owned claim this pack can carry into a room: a count of layers, a list of outcomes, and a role as technical control. The names of the layers are not on that press release.

IBM quotes a Gartner glossary that speaks of governance, trustworthiness, fairness, reliability, robustness, efficacy, and data protection. IBM's own categories on that page are governance, AI data protection and classification, and runtime inspection and enforcement [S8]. The TDD conceptual view reads four features from the same IBM account: an AI catalog, AI data mapping, continuous assurance, and runtime inspection. Those features are capabilities C1 to C4 on the conceptual view. C5 to C8 are derived. Treat C5 to C8 as the pack's proposal.

Four pillars, secondary

AWS Builder and Zscaler both name four pillars and attribute the framework to Gartner [S9] [S10]:

Pillar Job in the secondary crosswalk
Explainability and model monitoring Interpret outputs. Watch drift and behavior.
ModelOps Version, release, retrain, and retire models.
AI application security Protect the application that calls the model.
Privacy Protect sensitive data in training, prompts, retrieval, and outputs.

Zscaler also notes that some teams add regulatory compliance as a fifth pillar [S9]. E1A is partial. The pillar names are widely asserted. A Gartner-owned page that lists them was not found.

Four layers, secondary names

Use the layers when you need a place to put a control. Say the names are secondary.

Mindgard and PointGuard repeat the same four names. They are still secondary. The Market Guide PDF remains paywalled. E1B is partial.

NIST seven characteristics and four functions

NIST AI RMF 1.0 is voluntary guidance [S2].

Functions, in the order the framework uses them:

  1. Govern. Culture, policy, and accountability.
  2. Map. Context of the AI system and the risks that context creates.
  3. Measure. Analyze, assess, and track those risks.
  4. Manage. Prioritize risks and act on them.

Characteristics:

  1. Valid and reliable
  2. Safe
  3. Secure and resilient
  4. Accountable and transparent
  5. Explainable and interpretable
  6. Privacy-enhanced
  7. Fair with harmful bias managed

NIST AI 600-1 is the July 2024 Generative AI Profile. It is a voluntary companion to AI RMF 1.0 [S3]. Use it when the system is generative. Do not recite risk-table rows. This pack has not mined them. Debate 4, which asks whether the pillars cover the seven characteristics, stays open.

ISO/IEC 42001

ISO/IEC 42001:2023 is the first AI management-system standard. It specifies requirements for an AI management system, an AIMS [S5].

Certification is voluntary. ISO does not certify organizations. Independent certification bodies do [S14]. ANAB accredits those bodies for ISO/IEC 42001 and cites ISO/IEC 42006:2025 as the requirement on the bodies themselves [S15].

The NIST crosswalk maps AI RMF subcategories onto 42001 clauses and controls. The claims note records the example Govern 6.1 to B.10.2 [S4]. The crosswalk is a mapping table. It does not merge the two documents into one certificate.

EU AI Act risk tiers

Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 is binding law where it applies. It was signed 13 Jun 2024. Entry into force is 20 days after publication in the Official Journal. The desk pass records these dates [S6]:

Marker Date in the desk pass
Earlier phase 2 Feb 2025
Earlier phase 2 Aug 2025
General application 2 Aug 2026
Art. 6(1) high-risk path 2 Aug 2027

Classes recorded from the read, without annex detail:

Class Where the Act puts it
Prohibited practices Art. 5
High-risk AI systems Chapter III
GPAI models Chapter V
Transparency duties Art. 50

Do not assign a live use case to high-risk from memory. Annex III was not extracted. GPAI in this pack means the Chapter V category. The article definition was not copied out.

Retrieval check

What does the Gartner press release say about AI TRiSM's structure, and what does it leave out?
Answer

It says AI TRiSM comprises four layers of technical capabilities and lists outcomes: governance, trustworthiness, fairness, safety, reliability, security, privacy, and data protection. It does not name the four layers [S1].

Who can certify an organization to ISO/IEC 42001, and what route does NIST AI RMF offer?
Answer

Independent certification bodies certify the AIMS. ISO does not certify. ANAB accredits the bodies [S14] [S15]. NIST AI RMF 1.0 and AI 600-1 are voluntary. The desk pass found no NIST certification scheme [S2] [S3].

Which EU AI Act dates and classes are safe to say from this pack?
Answer

Prohibited practices Art. 5, high-risk Chapter III, GPAI Chapter V, transparency Art. 50. Phases include 2 Feb 2025, 2 Aug 2025, general application 2 Aug 2026, and the Art. 6(1) path on 2 Aug 2027 [S6]. Annex text was not extracted.
