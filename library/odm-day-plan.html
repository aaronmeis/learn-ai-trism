Business Explanation

Business Explanation

The day produces a design-review defense, not a pile of notes. Six focused blocks build the vocabulary, the arguments, and the one-pager. A short closed-book review sits before the application block so weak spots show up while there is still time to fix the page.

Focus total: 350 minutes. Wall clock below is about 6 hours 20 minutes with the two planned breaks.

Desk only. No installs. Ledger: source-ledger. Debates: CLAIMS-AND-DEBATES.

Schedule

Order Block Minutes Clock Exit, short
1 Core concepts 60 09:00 to 10:00 Four authorities stated from memory, with source grades
2 Mental models 45 10:00 to 10:45 Debates 1 to 3 decided in writing, Debate 4 left open
3 Active practice 90 10:45 to 12:15 Three desk maps done before the folded keys are opened
4 Teach it 30 12:35 to 13:05 Five-minute talk delivered once without notes
5 Edge cases 45 13:05 to 13:50 Five traps each have a room sentence
6 Spaced review 20 14:00 to 14:20 Score written in the Progress Log
7 Application 60 14:20 to 15:20 One-pager defended aloud for 90 seconds

Breaks

  • 12:15 to 12:35, 20 minutes, after practice.
  • 13:50 to 14:00, 10 minutes, after edge cases.

Exit criteria by block

Block 1, 09:00 to 10:00

  • You can say what [S1] publishes about structure, including the missing names.
  • You can list the four secondary pillars and the four secondary layers, each tagged secondary.
  • You can list NIST's seven characteristics and the four functions [S2].
  • You can say who certifies ISO/IEC 42001 and that NIST's documents in this ledger are voluntary [S3] [S14] [S15].
  • You can name the EU risk classes and dates recorded in the desk pass [S6].

Block 2, 10:00 to 10:45

  • A two-column note says what pillars are for and what layers are for.
  • Debate 2 ends with a written anchor: ISO/IEC 42001 plus NIST, TRiSM as the tool map.
  • Debate 3 ends with an order: red team, outer inspection, identity and least privilege.
  • Debate 4 is marked partial. You did not invent a fairness pillar.

Block 3, 10:45 to 12:15

  • One use case is placed on all four layers.
  • OWASP LLM01 to LLM10 are placed, with LLM01 mitigations separated from name-only risks.
  • Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage each have at least one control owner.
  • Folded keys were opened only after your own map was written.

Block 4, 12:35 to 13:05

  • The five-minute script was spoken once, timed.
  • You answered at least four reviewer questions from the block without opening the ledger.
  • One answer included the vendor-interest label on [S12].

Block 5, 13:05 to 13:50

  • Guardrails-only, shadow AI, over-permissioned agents, paywalled terms, and vendor interest each have a one-sentence defense.
  • You listed three things the pack still cannot claim.

Block 6, 14:00 to 14:20

  • Closed-book items from quiz-bank were answered before any block was reopened.
  • Score and misses are in Progress Log on the multi-pass table.
  • A Study 2 date is written. Weak score means about two days. Strong score can wait longer.

Block 7, 14:20 to 15:20

  • The one-pager names layers, standards anchors, and tool categories.
  • No product SKU is required for the design to make sense.
  • A 90-second defense was spoken. Claims you will not make are on the page.
