NIST AI 600-1 — twelve GAI risks (Section 2) [S3]

Read from the PDF (Firecrawl, first ~50 pages + query). The profile is voluntary. It defines risks novel to or exacerbated by generative AI, then maps suggested actions to AI RMF functions and to the seven trustworthiness characteristics (Section 3 / Appendix tables).

# Risk name One-line meaning
1 CBRN Information or Capabilities Easier access to or synthesis of chemical, biological, radiological, nuclear threat knowledge
2 Confabulation Confidently stated false content (hallucinations / fabrications)
3 Dangerous, Violent, or Hateful Content Violent, radicalizing, self-harm, illegal-activity content
4 Data Privacy Leakage or misuse of PII / sensitive data
5 Environmental Impacts High compute and related ecosystem impacts
6 Harmful Bias or Homogenization Amplified bias, subgroup disparities, undesired homogeneity
7 Human-AI Configuration Over-reliance, automation bias, anthropomorphizing, emotional entanglement
8 Information Integrity Dis/misinformation at scale; fact vs fiction blurred
9 Information Security Offensive cyber ease; larger attack surface on models/data/weights
10 Intellectual Property Unauthorized copyrighted / trade-secret content
11 Obscene, Degrading, and/or Abusive Content Including synthetic CSAM and nonconsensual intimate images
12 Value Chain and Component Integration Opaque third-party components, weak supplier vetting, lost accountability

E4W — must every characteristic have a separate control?

Run 2026-11-09. AI 600-1 and AI RMF treat the seven characteristics as socio-technical properties to manage across Govern/Map/Measure/Manage. Risks are mapped to characteristics; the text does not require one dedicated control per characteristic. That weakens a hard reading of Claim B that "no named pillar for X" automatically means a framework failure. Gaps in pillar names still matter for ownership clarity in a room.

E4B — draft map of 12 GAI risks → four secondary pillars [S9] [S10]

Pillar names remain secondary. This is an architect worksheet, not a Gartner finding.

Unmapped or weak under pillar names alone: Environmental Impacts; parts of Human-AI Configuration; CBRN and safety-coded content; Information Integrity at ecosystem scale; fairness as a named property. Layer view helps more for placement (governance / runtime / information / infrastructure) than pillars alone.
