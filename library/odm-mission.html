Mission for AI-TRISM

Business Explanation

Business Explanation

The day is done when an architect can defend a vendor-neutral reference architecture in a design review. That defense explains where controls sit, which standard or law they answer to, and which tool categories do the work. A market slogan is not the finish line.

Why

One focused day to reach working fluency for a design review on AI TRiSM.

Success looks like

  • Can separate AI TRiSM as a market category from NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, and the EU AI Act, including what the Gartner press release does and does not name
  • Can teach the four-layer placement and the pillar crosswalk in 5 minutes, with pillar names and layer names tagged secondary
  • Can defend a vendor-neutral one-page reference architecture: layers, standards anchors, tool categories, and a risk map a reviewer can challenge

Out of scope

  • Multi-week academy depth
  • Certification dump without practice
  • Training models, or a single-vendor product tutorial
  • Quoting the paywalled Market Guide as if this pack had read it
  • Any finding from Ray 2026 while that paper stays unread

Constraints

  • Time box: about 350 focused minutes
  • Ground claims in source-ledger and CLAIMS-AND-DEBATES
  • Desk only. No installs
