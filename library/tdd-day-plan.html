TDD day plan

Eight-block, about seven hour study day for the AI-TRISM architecture views.

Day plan: AI-TRISM

Focus: 420 minutes (7h) plus breaks. A 08:30 start finishes the teach-back about 16:15.

Business Explanation

Business Explanation

The day teaches an architect how to place AI trust, risk and security controls without pretending a market category is a standard. Morning blocks separate what the organization needs from how components talk and where they would run. Afternoon blocks cover outside systems, a desk-only build order, the ways those controls fail, and a short design review. Exit lines point at the view notes so the day cannot be "finished" by skimming the schedule.
